Vibe Discovery

Hai-Yang Wang, Chang Sun California Institute of Technology

AI coding agents decompose community astronomy codes into reusable modular bricks and join more than a dozen public ground and space archives—including Vera C. Rubin Observatory, ESA Euclid, DECam, Gaia, Legacy Surveys, and SPHEREx—to uncover distant Solar System worlds and faint nearby stars that single-survey pipelines miss.
