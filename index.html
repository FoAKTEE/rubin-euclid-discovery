Rubin / Euclid Multi-Archive Discovery Portal

Searches assembled across Vera C. Rubin Observatory, ESA Euclid, DECam and a dozen further public archives proposed 157 Solar System candidates. An independent audit regraded every claim from its own archive queries, orbit fits and pixels. This portal shows what survived, what did not, and the archival images behind each target.

Eyes on the Cosmos 3D Explorer & Studio

Solar System and Milky Way outreach explorer. The Sun, planets and Pluto follow an N-body integration started from JPL ephemeris state vectors, set in a Gaia DR3 Milky Way with black-hole disks, a WGS-84 Earth model and NORAD satellites. It holds 265 catalogued bodies; the 93 audited candidates are an opt-in survey layer, hidden until selected. Fitted orbits are display solutions for short arcs.

The Euclid track once listed as EUC-G18 is the catalogued main-belt asteroid 2019 UM31. It serves as a recovery control and is not a discovery.

Image data: ESA Euclid DR1 (VIS and MER), CTIO DECam via the NOIRLab Astro Data Archive, Vera C. Rubin Observatory DP1 and DP2, and Legacy Surveys DR10. Coadd frames are stamped with the midpoint of their epoch span or labelled as static sky.

This site publishes results and data products. The source code of the search pipelines is kept in a private repository.
